This question comes up in almost every engagement, and firms usually jump straight to drafting a disclosure policy. Start earlier than that.

Read your contracts first. Subconsultant clauses, confidentiality terms, and work product language may already answer the question before you decide what you want to say.

Then give your principals one rehearsed answer. Not a policy document. A short, honest sentence that everyone in the firm can deliver the same way.

Here is the part firms miss. Clients are not asking for a tool inventory. They want to know a licensed engineer still reviews and signs the work. That is accountability, and it is a much easier thing to say clearly.
