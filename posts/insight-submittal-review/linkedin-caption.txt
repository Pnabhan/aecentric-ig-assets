The fear with automating submittal review is that a tool starts approving things. That is not the design we recommend.

Split the work into five stages: intake, classification, spec check, risk flagging, and routing. The tool never marks a submittal approved. It sorts and surfaces, and it stops there.

Four rules keep it honest. No auto approval, no silent overrides, a full audit trail, and mandatory human sign off on anything flagged.

What you get back is attention. Low risk, repetitive submittals move fast, and your engineers spend their judgment on the ones that actually need it.

AI can sort a queue. It cannot carry your stamp or your liability.
