A report from ACEC Research Institute asks a question worth sitting with: is AI adoption at your firm being run as an IT project, or as a leadership one?

Leading Through AI Risk, published August 19, 2026, is built on interviews with 21 leaders spanning engineering firms, project owners, technology vendors, insurers and regulators. The report's own finding is blunt: firms that hand AI off to IT and call it done are managing the wrong risk. The decisions that actually matter, which workflows change, who is accountable for AI-assisted output, how governance gets built, sit with firm leadership, not with a software rollout.

We are curious how this plays out across the industry. Where does the AI conversation actually live at your firm right now, and has that changed in the last year?
