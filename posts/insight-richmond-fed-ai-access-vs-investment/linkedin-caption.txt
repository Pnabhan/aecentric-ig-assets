89 percent of firms say their employees have access to AI tools. Far fewer are putting capital behind it.

The Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond asked 255 firms in its June 2026 business surveys and published the results August 6, 2026. 89 percent said employees had access to AI tools, and 83 percent used AI in at least one formal business operation.

The capital side looks different. Among firms that grew capital investment last year, 40 percent put none of it into AI, and another 30 percent put only 1 to 9 percent there.

The survey breaks out 24 construction firms on next year's plans: 20.8 percent expect no AI investment, 62.5 percent expect AI to take 1 to 9 percent of capex, and 12.5 percent expect 10 to 24 percent. A small sample, so read it as direction, not a benchmark.

Giving people a tool and funding it are two separate decisions. Most firms have made the first one. If that is where your firm is, you are in good company, and the next question is where a capital dollar would do useful work on a project.
