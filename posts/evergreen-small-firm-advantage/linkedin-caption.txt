We've noticed a consistent pattern across the firms we advise: the smaller ones are moving faster on AI, and it has nothing to do with budget.

A 20 person firm can make a decision on Monday, start a pilot by Wednesday, and update its standards by the end of the month. A 2,000 person firm needs a committee to approve each of those same three steps, and by the time it convenes, the smaller firm already has results to show.

The advantage is decision speed, not resources. If you run a larger firm, the fix is not waiting for a firm wide program to get organized. Give one office or one studio the authority to run its own pilot and report back. Let the results, not a steering committee, decide what scales next.
