At AECentric, we have one test for whether AI adoption is actually working inside a firm: not the tools purchased, not the pilots launched, but whether the team is producing more this year than it did last year.

Those first two are easy to point to. A license count and a pilot program both look like progress on a slide, and both can sit there doing very little. The number that actually tells you something is harder to fake: output, measured against the same team, twelve months apart.

We hold our own work to that standard, and we think every AEC firm evaluating AI investment should too. Tools and pilots are inputs. Team capacity is the result.
