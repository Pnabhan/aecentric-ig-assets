Judgment used to come from repetition. Juniors checked hundreds of beam sizes, redlined drawings, and ran the same calc until the numbers started to feel right or wrong before the answer even printed.

AI removes the reps. It does not remove the need for judgment.

When a tool hands over an answer in seconds, a junior sees the output but skips the struggle of getting it wrong first. That struggle is where judgment actually gets built, and it has no obvious replacement.

The fix is not keeping AI away from junior staff. It is making them defend, break, and rebuild what the tool produces, so they still earn what used to come free with the grunt work.

Train judgment on purpose, or lose it by accident.
