79 percent of AEC firms plan significant investments in AI and automation in 2026. 8 percent have a regularly updated, enterprise wide technology roadmap.

That is from Zweig Group's 2026 Information Technology Report of AEC Firms, released October 21, 2026. The same release says only 6 percent of firms have fully embedded AI into operations and 54 percent are still actively exploring it.

The budget split tells the rest of the story. Software is 36.7 percent of IT budgets. Training is 0.8 percent.

Zweig does not publish how many firms took part, so treat these as one benchmark, not a census. But the pattern is easy to recognize: buy the tool, skip the plan, and leave the people who have to use it on their own.

The fix is the order. Write the roadmap first, size the training line second, pick the software last.
