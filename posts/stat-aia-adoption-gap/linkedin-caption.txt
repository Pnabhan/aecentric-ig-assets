AIA research puts it at 8 percent. That is the share of architecture firms that have actually implemented AI in their practice. Another 20 percent are working on it, and only 6 percent of the profession uses it regularly.

In the same study, 90 percent reported concerns about accuracy, security, and transparency.

Read those two numbers together. The worry is nearly universal. The adoption is not. Most firms are still deciding, which means the concerns have not been answered so much as postponed.

The firms moving now are not the ones with fewer concerns. They are the ones who wrote down what would have to be true to proceed, and then went and checked.

Source: AIA, Artificial Intelligence Adoption in Architecture Firms, March 2025.
