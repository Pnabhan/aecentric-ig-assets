Buying enterprise licenses is the easy part. The month after is what decides whether anything changes.

Start narrow. Week one, name one repeatable task your team already does every week. Firm wide enthusiasm is not a rollout plan, and a tool without a target workflow becomes a novelty by day ten.

Week two, load your standards. Give it your terminology, your templates, and examples of output you have already approved, so first drafts start in your firm's voice instead of a generic one.

Weeks three and four, measure. Track hours on that one task before and after. When you go back to leadership for the next phase, a number beats a survey every time.
