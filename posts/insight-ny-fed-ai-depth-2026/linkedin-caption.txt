In the New York Fed's August 2026 surveys, 61 percent of service firms and 51 percent of manufacturers in New York and Northern New Jersey said they used AI in the past six months. Among those users, the median share of workers actually using it was 17 percent at service firms and 7 percent at manufacturers, and only 15 percent of service firms had committed significant resources.

We read that as a useful baseline for AEC owners who feel behind: adoption is wide, but inside most firms it is thin. Count real weekly use and funding before comparing yourself to the headline.

Source: Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Liberty Street Economics, September 1, 2026.
