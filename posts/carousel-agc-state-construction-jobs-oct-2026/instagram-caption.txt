Construction employment grew in 37 states and DC between October 2025 and October 2026, according to @agcofamerica's analysis of BLS data. Texas added the most jobs, 26,900. Louisiana grew the fastest at 13.0%, adding 17,600. Ohio and Illinois both grew close to 7%. Thirteen states lost jobs, led by Georgia at 6,300.

For design firms these numbers trail our own pipeline, because our work starts before the crews show up. If your market is on the growth list, the capacity question is already on your desk.

Source: AGC of America, analysis of BLS state construction employment, November 20, 2026.
