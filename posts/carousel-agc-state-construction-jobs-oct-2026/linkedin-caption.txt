Louisiana grew construction employment 13% in one year. Texas added the most jobs, 26,900, but on a much bigger base.

AGC of America published the state numbers on November 20, from BLS data for October 2025 to October 2026. Thirty-seven states and DC added construction jobs. Thirteen lost them, with Georgia, New Jersey and New York down the most. Tennessee does not appear on any of AGC's top-five lists.

For design firms, field hiring is a trailing signal. Our work shows up first, which means the firms in the growth states are already feeling the capacity squeeze in their own offices.

The ones who can produce more per person will get to choose which projects they chase.
