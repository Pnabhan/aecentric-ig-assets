Construction input prices rose 11.9% in the 12 months to September 2026, the largest increase in four years. Over the same period, the producer price index for new nonresidential buildings, a measure of what contractors report they would charge, rose 5.4%.

That spread, according to AGC of America's analysis of BLS data, is driven by sharp increases in diesel (up 115.2%), copper and brass mill shapes (up 30.0%), steel mill products (up 27.6%) and aluminum mill shapes (up 25.2%).

For firms across design and construction, it means margins are under pressure before any technology investment is on the table. We think that is the right lens for evaluating AI tools right now: start with where they save time and cost on work you already do.

Sources: AGC of America, October 15, 2026, and AGC Data DIGest, October 16, 2026.
