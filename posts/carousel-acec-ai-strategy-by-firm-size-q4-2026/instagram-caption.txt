57% of engineering firms now have an AI strategy in place, according to the ACEC Research Institute's Q4 2026 Engineering Business Sentiment report. The split by size is the real story: 87% of firms with more than 500 FTEs, and 16% of firms with 1 to 25.

I understand why. A 20-person firm has no innovation team and no spare hours. But a strategy at that size fits on one page: which tasks you will try AI on, who checks the output, and what data never leaves the building.

Source: ACEC Research Institute, Engineering Business Sentiment Q4 2026, published November 18, 2026, fielded October 13 to 28 with 643 completed surveys from ACEC member firms. The 2024 Q1 and 2026 Q1 figures on slide 2 are derived from the point changes ACEC reports.
