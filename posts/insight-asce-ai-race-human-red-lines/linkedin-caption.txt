ASCE's AI Roadmap for Application in Civil Engineering, AI RACE, which secured support from the ASCE Board of Direction on October 17, 2026, sets five human red lines for AI in practice. The first: AI does not hold responsible charge or seal, sign, or certify engineering work. The second: no AI output is relied on until a competent person has verified it, in proportion to the consequence of error, and that use is recorded.

We think those two lines belong in every engineering firm's AI policy, word for word.

Source: ASCE, AI RACE, October 2026.
