Using AI is not the same as changing anything.

Adopting a tool is one project. Trusting the data under it is another. Moving a number is the one that counts.

I watch firms treat the first step as the finish line. Everyone gets a login, a few people run a pilot, and the story stops there. But a login is not trust, and trust is not a result you can point to on a live project.

The firms that actually change something keep going past step one. They check the output against work a senior person already reviewed. They find where it is wrong and fix the process, not just the prompt. Then they measure something real: hours saved on a task, fewer review rounds, a schedule that held.

If you cannot name the number that moved, you have adopted a tool. You have not changed anything yet.
