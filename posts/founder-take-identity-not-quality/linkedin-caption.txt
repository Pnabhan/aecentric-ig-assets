When a firm pushes back on AI, the stated reason is usually quality. That is rarely the whole story.

More often it is identity. For a lot of experienced people the work is the craft, and a tool that does part of it quickly can feel like it is taking something rather than giving it back. That reaction deserves respect, not an argument.

What we have seen work is simple. Show the time it actually gives back on a task they already dislike, let them keep control of the review, and then let them choose. People adopt tools they were invited to, not ones they were handed.
