More people are leaving construction jobs than a year ago. The BLS JOLTS release for September 2026 shows 198,000 construction quits, up from 127,000 in September 2025. Job openings climbed from 206,000 to 298,000. Monthly hires barely moved, 337,000 to 356,000.

These are industry-wide numbers, not design firms, and September is preliminary. But every project lead knows the pattern. When someone leaves, what they knew about the job leaves with them.

I think that is the most practical case for AI on a project team. Not replacing people. Making sure the next person can find what the last one knew.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, JOLTS, released November 3, 2026, Tables 1, 2 and 4, construction, seasonally adjusted.
