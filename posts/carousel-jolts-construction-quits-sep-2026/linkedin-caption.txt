Construction quits were up 56% in a year. Hires were up about 6%.

That is from the BLS JOLTS release on November 3. In September 2026, 198,000 construction workers quit, against 127,000 a year earlier. Openings went from 206,000 to 298,000. The numbers are industry-wide and preliminary, but they match a conversation I keep having with project leaders: the hard part is not finding work, it is holding the team together long enough to deliver it.

When someone walks off a job, what they knew walks with them. The RFI history, the owner's preferences, the reason a detail changed. Most of that lives in inboxes and in people's heads.

That is where I think AI earns its place first on a project team. Not fewer people. Less lost every time one leaves.
