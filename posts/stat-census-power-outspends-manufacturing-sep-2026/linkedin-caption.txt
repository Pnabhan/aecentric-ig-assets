Private power construction reached a $171.7 billion annual rate in September 2026, up 12.1% in a year. Private manufacturing construction fell 17.9% to $167.2 billion. Power now outspends factories, while total U.S. construction spending was down 2.7%, according to the Census Bureau's November 2 release.

For design and engineering firms, that is a shift in where the work is. Power projects are heavy on utility coordination, interconnection studies and long-lead equipment schedules.

That is a practical place to apply AI: faster first drafts of study reports, submittal reviews against specifications, and tracking equipment lead times across a portfolio. Figures are preliminary and not adjusted for prices.
