Private power construction ran at $171.7 billion a year in September 2026, up 12.1% from a year earlier. Private manufacturing construction fell 17.9% to $167.2 billion over the same period. Power now outspends factories, while total construction was down 2.7%, per the Census Bureau's September release.

For AEC firms, this is where the work is moving. Power construction is growing while factory construction cools. Power projects lean hard on utility coordination, interconnection studies and long-lead equipment, so firms that can turn around studies and submittals faster will win more of them.

Figures are preliminary, seasonally adjusted annual rates, not adjusted for prices.
