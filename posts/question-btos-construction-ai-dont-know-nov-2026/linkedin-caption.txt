One in four U.S. construction businesses told the Census Bureau they do not know whether they will be using AI in the next six months. In the survey published November 19, 25.3% said do not know, 57.2% said no and 17.5% said yes. For professional, scientific and technical services, which include engineering and architecture firms, 44.8% said yes.

The undecided group is the interesting one. A do-not-know answer usually means no one inside the business has been asked to make the call.

It does not take a large plan to change that. Pick one task to test, give one person ownership of the test, and set a review date. Census now words the question as Super Intelligence, formerly AI, so expect that label in future releases.

Who at your firm owns the AI decision today?
