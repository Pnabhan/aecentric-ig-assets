One in four U.S. construction businesses, 25.3%, told the Census Bureau they do not know whether they will be using AI in the next six months. Another 57.2% said no and 17.5% said yes. Among professional, scientific and technical services firms, which include engineering and architecture, 44.8% said yes.

"Do not know" is the most useful answer in that list. It usually means nobody has been asked to decide. For an AEC firm, a short answer is enough to start: one task to test, one person who owns the test, and a date to review what happened.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, Business Trends and Outlook Survey, published November 19, 2026, collected November 2 to 15. Census now words the question as "Super Intelligence (formerly AI)".
