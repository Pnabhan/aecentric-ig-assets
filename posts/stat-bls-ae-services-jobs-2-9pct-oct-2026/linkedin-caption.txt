Architecture and engineering services firms added 50,200 jobs in the year to October 2026, according to the BLS Employment Situation released November 6. That is growth of 2.9%, against about 0.6% for all U.S. payrolls.

For firm leaders, the number describes the market you hire in. Design and engineering talent is still in demand faster than the economy is growing, which keeps every open seat expensive and slow to fill.

That is why we suggest starting AI adoption with capacity, not headcount. Look at where your current team spends hours on first drafts, document review and repetitive coordination, and test tools there first. October figures are preliminary and the percentages are our calculation from BLS Table B-1.

Which task would you hand off first if you could get those hours back?
