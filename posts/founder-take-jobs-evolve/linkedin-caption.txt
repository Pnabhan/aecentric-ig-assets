Worried AI will take your job? History is not on the side of that fear.

A century ago most people in this country worked in farming. Technology moved that work, and new jobs replaced it, in categories nobody at the time could have named. The transition was real and it was hard for the people living through it. It was not the end of work.

AI is the next shift, not the end of the story. The engineers who do well will be the ones who move toward it early enough to shape how it gets used in their firm, rather than having it arrive fully formed around them.
