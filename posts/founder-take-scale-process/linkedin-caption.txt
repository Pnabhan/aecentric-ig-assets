AI does not fix a broken business. It scales whatever is already there.

We see firms buy a license expecting it to clean up pricing that undersells the work, scope that keeps drifting after the contract is signed, or handoffs nobody has ever mapped out. None of that changes because a tool got faster. Those are still ordinary business problems, and they are still the firm's to fix.

What AI does well is scale a process that is already sound. Fix the process first, then let AI carry more of it.
