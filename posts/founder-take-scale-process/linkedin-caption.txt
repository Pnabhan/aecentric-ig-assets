AI gives you leverage. Be careful what you point it at.

A tool does not fix a broken process. It scales it. If your review steps are unclear, your file naming is improvised, and nobody is sure who signs off, adding AI does not clean that up. It produces the same confusion faster and in greater volume.

Scale what is already structured and repeatable. That is where the leverage is real and the result is predictable.

Everything else is worth fixing first, and the fixing is usually cheaper than the tool.
