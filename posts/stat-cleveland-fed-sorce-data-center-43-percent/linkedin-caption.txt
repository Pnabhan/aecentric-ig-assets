43% of firms in a Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland survey said they work on data center construction, directly or as a contractor or subcontractor. Among the 80 firms involved, 26% provide professional and consulting services, the part of the market where engineering and design firms work.

The same Fourth District survey, published October 14, 2026, found 81% of firms used AI in the past six months. Among AI users, 11% said it increased staffing and 2% said it decreased it, with most reporting no change.

For AEC firms, data centers show up as both a client and a reason to work faster. A practical step is to look at which data center deliverables your team repeats most, and test AI on the first draft of one of them.
