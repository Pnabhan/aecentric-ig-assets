The Federal Reserve's November Beige Book, released November 25, carried a blunt line from the Philadelphia District: other than data center construction, contacts reported that nonresidential construction activity is declining. One architect said slow demand may require their firm to consider layoffs.

That is one district and a handful of contacts, not a national count. But it describes a pattern many AEC firms will recognize: a strong market in one project type can hide a softening one everywhere else. A practical check is to sort your backlog by market and see how much of next year rests on a single sector.

Source: Federal Reserve Beige Book, November 2026, Philadelphia District, information collected on or before November 16, 2026.
