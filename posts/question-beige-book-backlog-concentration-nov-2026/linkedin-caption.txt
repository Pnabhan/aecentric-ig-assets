The Federal Reserve's November Beige Book included this from the Philadelphia District: other than data center construction, contacts reported that nonresidential construction activity is declining, and one architect said slow demand may require their firm to consider layoffs.

It is one district and a qualitative report, not a national statistic. Still, it points to a risk worth checking in any AEC firm right now. A strong run in one project type can mask a slowdown everywhere else in the backlog.

A simple exercise helps: sort next year's backlog by market sector and client, then look at how much rests on the top one. AI tools can speed up the pursuit work needed to rebalance, from proposal first drafts to market research, but the decision starts with seeing the concentration.
