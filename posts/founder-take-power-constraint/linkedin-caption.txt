In data center development, power is the real constraint. Not land, not capital, not design capacity.

Capital and land chase power, not the other way around. That single fact reorders how you evaluate a site, and it is why secondary markets keep winning deals that on paper should have gone somewhere larger. If a market can energize sooner, it moves to the front.

The developers getting this right have started thinking like energy companies. They talk to utilities early, they understand interconnection queues, and they treat a power position as the asset it has become.
