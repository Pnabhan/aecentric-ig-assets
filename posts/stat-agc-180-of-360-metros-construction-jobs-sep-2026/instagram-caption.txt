Construction employment rose in 180 of 360 U.S. metro areas from September 2025 to September 2026, fell in 118 and held steady in 62, according to @agcofamerica's analysis of federal data. AGC noted a correlation between the gains and where data centers and large power and manufacturing facilities are being built.

For AEC firms, the national average hides a split market. A firm in a growing metro is fighting for people, while one a few hours away may be watching its backlog thin. Knowing which side your market is on should shape whether this year's focus is capacity or new work.

Source: AGC of America, October 28, 2026, analysis of BLS metro employment data.
