Half of U.S. metro areas added construction jobs over the past year. AGC of America's October 28 analysis of federal data found employment rose in 180 of 360 metros from September 2025 to September 2026, fell in 118 and held steady in 62. AGC noted a correlation between the gains and where data centers and large power and manufacturing facilities are being built.

For design and engineering firms, that split matters more than the national number. In a growing metro, the constraint is capacity and the question is how to deliver more with the team you have. In a shrinking one, the constraint is pipeline and the question is which markets to pursue next.

Both answers start with knowing where your own hours go.

Which side of the split is your market on?
