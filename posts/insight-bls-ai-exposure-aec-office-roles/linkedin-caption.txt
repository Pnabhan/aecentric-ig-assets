The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics now rates every occupation for AI exposure. In AEC, the exposure sits in the office, not on the jobsite.

BLS published the ratings on August 27, 2026 with its 2025 to 2035 employment projections, sorting 831 occupations into four relative groups: Very high, High, Moderate and Low. By our count of the BLS table, 35 of the 36 architecture and engineering occupations rate High or Very high and none rate Low. In construction and extraction, 53 of 60 rate Low.

Architects, civil engineers, drafters and construction managers all rate High. BLS is explicit that exposure "does not imply job loss, productivity gains, automation probability, or wage effects", and it still projects construction manager jobs to grow 9.1 percent, from 609,100 to 664,300.

For an AEC firm, that points the AI question at your licensed and technical staff: the people who design, specify, check and sign. That is where to test new workflows first, with professional review kept where it belongs.
