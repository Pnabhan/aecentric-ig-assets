Build once, deploy many times.

We see this same gap across the firms we advise. Someone on the team is genuinely good at getting a strong answer out of an AI tool. Far fewer of those firms have turned that skill into a workflow the rest of the team can actually run.

A one off answer helps the project in front of you. A workflow helps every project that comes after it, without depending on the one person who figured it out first. That is the real difference between a team that looks busy and one that is compounding its time.

If the wins live in one person's head, they are not wins yet, they are anecdotes.
