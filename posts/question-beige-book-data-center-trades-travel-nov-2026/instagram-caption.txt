In the Federal Reserve's November Beige Book, a North Carolina builder in the Richmond District attributed wage pressure partly to trades workers' willingness to travel out of state to work on data center projects for higher wages.

It is one builder's report, not a survey. But the pull is easy to picture for AEC firms too. Data center programs pay well and move fast, and they recruit from the same pool of field staff, inspectors and project engineers everyone else needs. Retention plans that rely on pay alone are hard to sustain against that, which puts more weight on the work itself: less rework, less paperwork, clearer handoffs.

Source: Federal Reserve Beige Book, November 2026, Richmond District, information collected on or before November 16, 2026.
